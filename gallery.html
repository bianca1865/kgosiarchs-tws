FEATURED WORK

Architecture with purpose.

A selection of projects shaped by context, function and thoughtful design.

Morula House

A contemporary residence designed around light, privacy and connection to the outdoors.

Brief

"Create a modern family home with generous communal spaces and quieter private areas."

Challenge

Balancing openness and natural light with privacy from the surrounding properties.

Result

A restrained residence organised around proportion, light and indoor-outdoor living.

Kgosi Office

A contemporary workplace focused on clarity, collaboration and a strong sense of arrival.

Brief

"Develop a professional workplace that supports both focused work and collaboration."

Challenge

Creating distinct working zones without making the relatively compact space feel closed.

Result

An efficient workplace with clear circulation, generous daylight and a calm material palette.

Metsi Residence

A quiet contemporary home where simple forms create a strong relationship with the landscape.

Brief

"Create a comfortable family residence that feels connected to its natural surroundings."

Challenge

Working with the site's orientation while maintaining privacy and outdoor connection.

Result

A balanced composition of solid and open spaces that brings landscape into everyday living.
